Why Japanese manga?

Manga is built to make readers feel what a character feels. That is exactly what values need.

Stories, not slogans

  • Your values appear as real dilemmas your people face, drawn from employee interviews.
  • Japanese panel flow and expressive faces make emotion readable in seconds.
  • A 24-page manga reads in about 15 minutes, in English, on any device.

What the research suggests

  • In one study, readers given a comic version of educational content scored higher on comprehension than those given text alone, when the images matched the content.
  • A 2023 meta-analysis of educational comics found moderate positive effects on learning and attitudes (studies were mostly in schools).
  • We design for discussion afterwards, which is where culture is really built.

Honest about AI

We use AI image tools to illustrate, which keeps projects fast and affordable. Every story, script, panel layout and final edit is done by a person. We will tell you exactly which tools we use, and our contract explains how rights work for AI-assisted art.

FAQ

Who owns the manga?

You do, to the fullest extent the law allows. We transfer rights to everything people create: the story, script, panel layout and edits. Under current US Copyright Office guidance, purely AI-generated images may not be protected by copyright on their own. We explain this clearly in the contract before you sign.

Will it look like our company?

Yes. We base characters and scenes on your workplace (office, factory floor, store, field) and your real situations. We never copy existing manga characters or depict real people without written consent.

Will employees find it cheesy?

Not if it is honest. We build stories from real employee interviews, with characters who struggle and make mistakes, rather than perfect heroes reciting slogans.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA on request and only put your materials into AI tools with your consent.

Can you do other languages?

English is standard. The Rollout Kit includes one more language, and Japanese is always available.
